Explain this – how foreign R&E is amortized (not used for the R&D credit)

Foreign research or experimental (R&E) expenditures are not Section 41 QREs and never enter the credit calculation. They are a separate Section 174 deduction-timing item: the current deduction under Section 174A applies only to domestic R&E, so foreign R&E must still be capitalized and amortized.

How the amortization works

  • Period: 15 years (180 months).
  • Start: the midpoint of the tax year the cost is paid or incurred (mid-year convention) – regardless of the month it was actually paid.
  • Result: year 1 = 1/30 of the cost; years 2–15 = 1/15 each; year 16 = the last 1/30.
  • Book vs. tax: books usually expense the full cost in year 1, so the difference is added back on the tax return (M-1 / M-3) and reverses over the next 15 years.
  • No acceleration: if the project is abandoned or the property disposed of, the remaining balance is generally not written off – amortization continues on the same schedule.
  • Reporting: the amortization is generally reported on Form 4562, Part VI (Section 174).
