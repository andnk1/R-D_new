Step 5 of 6 · Payroll tax option, Section 280C and the credit method.

A. Payroll-tax credit option

If the company qualifies, do you want the study to show the payroll-tax credit option?

This is a presentation choice, not an eligibility question. The app determines whether the company appears to meet the Qualified Small Business (QSB) tests from gross receipts, first year of receipts, controlled-group information, and prior payroll-election history.

Explain this – the payroll-tax credit election

A Qualified Small Business may elect to use part of the research credit against payroll taxes. The current maximum election is generally $500,000 per tax year, and an election cannot be made if an election was made for five or more preceding tax years. The election must be made on or before the due date of the originally filed income-tax return, including extensions.

IRS: Research credit against payroll tax for small businesses
Note: calculations are not currently included in the study calculation engine; additional calculations to be added soon. (Today the study shows the payroll option automatically when the gross-receipts test is met, the tax year is 2025 or 2026, and the return has not been filed.)
B. Section 280C reduced-credit election
Treatment appliedSection 280C reduced credit

No input needed. The app uses the filing-status information from step 1 and the study settings to show the selected / default treatment in the summary. If the return has already been filed, the filing-status answers determine whether the election was already made.

Explain this – Section 280C

The reduced-credit election lowers the credit rather than requiring a separate reduction of the related domestic R&E deduction / capital account. The election must be made on the original timely filed return, including extensions, and cannot generally be first made or changed on an amended return.

IRS: Instructions for Form 6765 – Section 280C
Note: calculations are not currently included in the study calculation engine; additional calculations to be added soon. (Today the engine applies the 280C reduced credit to every result.)
C. Credit calculation method
MethodDetermined by the app on the Review page

No input needed. The app calculates the methods supported by the available data – Regular Credit and / or Alternative Simplified Credit – and presents the applicable result. The preparer's job is to provide accurate current and historical data.

Explain this – how the method is chosen

The ASC generally depends heavily on the current year and prior three years of QREs. The Regular Credit can require gross-receipts and historical fixed-base information. Missing historical data should remain missing; it should not be silently converted to zero simply to produce a larger credit.
