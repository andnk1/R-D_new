Step 2 of 6 · What was being developed or improved.

Page purpose. Collect enough facts for the app to understand what was being developed or improved and whether the activity appears to satisfy the core Section 41 tests.

2026 and later: for tax years beginning after 2025, Form 6765 generally requires business-component information, subject to the form instructions and exceptions. Structuring the study around projects / business components now avoids rebuilding the data later.
FYI – The four-part test

All four of the following criteria must be met for the research activity to qualify.

1
Permitted Purpose
Business Component Test
Core requirement

The activity must aim to create a new or improved business component for sale, lease, license, or use in your trade or business.

The goal

The research must intend to improve the functionality, performance, reliability, or quality of that component.

Aesthetic, cosmetic, or seasonal design modifications do not qualify.
›
2
Elimination of Uncertainty
Section 174 Test
Core requirement

At the outset of the project, there must be a lack of information or technical uncertainty regarding the development of the component.

The focus

Uncertainty regarding the capability to develop it, the method to achieve it, or the appropriate final design.

The project does not have to succeed – attempting to resolve an uncertainty that ultimately fails still meets this test.
›
3
Process of Experimentation
Substantially all – 80% rule
Core requirement

The business must evaluate alternative solutions through a structured, systematic process.

The focus

Modeling, simulation, systematic trial-and-error, prototyping, or testing multiple design variations to resolve the technical uncertainties.

Under IRC §41(d)(1)(C), 80% or more of the research activities must constitute elements of this process of experimentation.
›
4
Technological in Nature
Hard sciences
Core requirement

The process of experimentation must fundamentally rely on the principles of the hard sciences.

The focus

Engineering, computer science, biological sciences, or physical sciences (such as chemistry or physics).

Social sciences, economics, humanities, or routine consumer surveys are excluded.
✓ Permitted Purpose  +  ✓ Uncertainty  +  ✓ Experimentation  +  ✓ Technological  =  Qualified research

Explain this – does the project have to succeed?

A project does not have to succeed to qualify. Failed or abandoned projects can still contain qualified research if the activity met the requirements while it was performed.
